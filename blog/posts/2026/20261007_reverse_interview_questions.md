# Reverse Interview Questions

A while ago I [wrote](20260727-what-i-want-to-become-when-i-grow-up/) about my values at work, and that if I had a choice, what would I choose. It is not often that I have that choice, but it is good to aim idealistic and expect realistic. So, in that line, I have also prepared a list of questions that I'd be asking in reverse interviews to get a signal regarding those values that I can expect at a job I'm offered but has not accepted yet. If you don't know what a reverse interview is read about it first, I don't have a good source handy. 

There are two things worth noting first:

- I'm going to ask the majority of these questions after I've got an offer to figure out the fit.
- My expectation (based on my experiences) is that most of the answers to these questions are unsatisfactory, but at least will give me an idea of what I'm walking into.

Each row below is one question, who to ask it of, the signal I read in the answer, and what a strong or weak answer looks like. It is grouped by the person I am asking, then by what I am probing for.
