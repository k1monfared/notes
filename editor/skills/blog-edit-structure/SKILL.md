# Blog Edit: Structure (phase 4)

Build the human narrative. Requires: locked spine in brief.log, points.log, clarity.log.

## Process

1. **Hook.** Does the opening start a conversation? It must not read like a paper abstract. Propose a fix if it does.
2. **Roadmap.** Does the reader know where the piece is going, in a talking voice, without a sales pitch and without dangling promises?
3. **Reorder.** Propose the order that serves the spine. Warmup and engine material first, spine material as the payoff, side points placed to keep the reader moving. Check the braid: if two threads run through the piece, signal early that they will meet.
4. **Payoff ledger.** List every hook with its payoff and every payoff with its setup. Broken entries are findings.
5. **Transitions.** Check each section hand-off. Sections must hand off, not stack.
6. **Title and headers.** Check the title against the promise. A provocative title is fine (standing aim); a misleading one is not. Audit headers: guide, not just label.
7. **Conclusion.** Check against the standing aim. On this blog the ending should open the question, not close it. An ending that answers too hard is a finding.
8. Write `structure.log` with the proposal and the ledger. **Present to the human for approval before any reorder is applied.**

## Worked example

For notes/blog/20260806_: spine is C (importance is social), B (process is the product) is warmup and engine. The B thread disappears for sections 3-8 and resurfaces late; the proposal must signal the braid early. Finding F01: the final paragraph answers the title question, which violates the standing aim.

## Edge cases

- Never apply the reorder yourself. Propose, get approval, then the reviser applies with changelog entries.
- If the spine is not locked, stop and ask the human.
